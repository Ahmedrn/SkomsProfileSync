# Changelog

## 1.2.0 (2026-10-31)

- One Skom's minimap button opens all your Skom's addons when two or more are installed (/skoms).
- New characters are offered your saved setups when they log in, until they have one.
- Setups include the addon list: the same addons are enabled, and their profiles switched after the reload.

## 1.1.1 (2026-10-26)

- New display name: Skom's Profile Sync.

## 1.1.0 (2026-10-23)

- Delete addon profiles by hand, with who uses each one, and quick picks for unused profiles or other servers.
- Clearer Save a setup tab.
- Fixes for action bars and full macro lists.
- New logo.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-21)

First release.

- Save a character as a setup and apply it to your alts: action bars, keybinds, macros, BindPad, addon profiles and game settings.
- Backups before every apply, with undo.
- Storage tab to clean up unused addon profiles.
